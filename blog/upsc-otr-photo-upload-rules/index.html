UPSC OTR 2026: Strict 10-Day Photo Rule, Dimensions & Signature Guidelines

Published: Oct 2026 | Category: UPSC Guide

Everything you need to know about UPSC Civil Services & NDA photo requirements: 20KB to 300KB, 350x350 to 1000x1000 pixels, and recent date imprint.

UPSC has updated its One Time Registration (OTR) rules for all competitive exams. ### UPSC 10-Day Photograph Rule: - The photograph must not be older than 10 days from the opening date of the online application form. - The face of the candidate should occupy 3/4th (75%) space of the photograph. - The candidate's name and the date of taking the photograph must be imprinted on the bottom of the photo. ### Dimension & Size Standards: - File Size: 20 KB to 300 KB. - Pixel Range: Minimum 350 × 350 pixels, Maximum 1000 × 1000 pixels (Default exact preset: 500 × 600 px). - Format: JPG/JPEG only.
