Top 7 Reasons Why Indian Exam Portals Reject Photos & How to Fix Them in 2026

Published: Oct 2026 | Category: Application Guides

Avoid rejection by UPSC, SSC, IBPS, and NTA. Learn the technical checks behind file size KB, dimensions, DPI, and background rules.

Every year, over 150,000 application forms across UPSC, SSC, IBPS, NEET, and State PSCs are rejected or withheld during scrutiny due to photo and signature upload discrepancies. ### 1. File Size Exceeding Portal Bounds Server scripts check the exact byte length before saving to AWS/NIC servers. If the rule says 20 KB to 50 KB, uploading 51.2 KB or 19.8 KB will immediately trigger a validation error. Our tool uses binary search quantization to guarantee your file lands precisely within the allowed range. ### 2. Incorrect Aspect Ratio and Pixel Dimensions Forcing an image into non-matching aspect ratios results in facial distortion (e.g. horizontally stretched or squeezed faces). Exam portals like Kerala PSC (150x200 px) or DSSSB (480x672 px) strictly reject incorrect pixel counts. ### 3. Missing Candidate Name & Date of Photo (DOP) SSC, UPPSC, TNPSC, and Kerala PSC mandate candidate name and date of photo printed at the bottom on a clean white strip. We include an automated Name & Date stamp feature that renders crisp typography according to official guidelines. ### 4. Background Color Violations White or light grey background is strictly enforced by NTA (NEET/JEE Main), GATE, and UPSC. Photos with colorful bedroom walls or outdoor greenery are rejected during computerized admit card generation. ### 5. Signature Written in Capital (BLOCK) Letters IBPS, SBI, and SSC explicitly declare: "Signature in Capital/Block Letters is NOT acceptable and will lead to rejection of application." Always sign in running handwriting. ### 6. Low Resolution & Shadow Casting on Mobile Photos Taking a mobile photo of your signature without flash often casts shadow from the phone. Our built-in Signature Cleaner removes paper grey tones and isolates crisp ink lines. ### 7. Incorrect DPI Tags Exams like TNPSC and IBPS require 200 DPI resolution tags in the JPEG JFIF header. Our compressor automatically injects 200/300 DPI markers directly into the exported JPEG file.
