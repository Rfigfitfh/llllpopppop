How to Scan & Resize Left Thumb Impression (20-50 KB) for IBPS & SBI Bank Exams

Published: Oct 2026 | Category: Banking Exams

Learn how to get crisp fingerprint ridges, remove ink smudges, and format to 240x240 pixels (20KB - 50KB) at 200 DPI.

Banking exams conducted by IBPS and State Bank of India require a digital upload of the Left Thumb Impression (LTI). ### Requirements: - Size: 20 KB to 50 KB. - Dimensions: Exact 240 × 240 pixels (3 cm × 3 cm). - Resolution: 200 DPI preferred. - Stamp Pad: Blue or Black ink pad on plain white sheet. ### Pro-Tips for Perfect Thumb Impression: - Press lightly on the stamp pad to avoid ink pooling, which obscures fingerprint loops and whorls. - Touch gently on paper without twisting or dragging your thumb. - Use our **3-Stage Biometric Thumb Impression Cleaner** tool to boost ridge sharpness and remove paper texture before uploading.
