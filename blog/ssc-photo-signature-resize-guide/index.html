SSC CGL, CHSL, MTS & GD: Complete Photo & Signature Specification Guide 2026

Published: Oct 2026 | Category: SSC Guide

Step-by-step tutorial on meeting SSC 20-50 KB photo rules, 10-20 KB signature rules, dimensions, and live webcam capture rules.

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) launched its new portal (ssc.gov.in) with enhanced automated image scrutiny. ### Official Specifications for SSC: - **Photograph**: 20.0 KB to 50.0 KB, JPEG/JPG format. - **Photo Dimensions**: 3.5 cm width × 4.5 cm height (exact 240 × 320 pixels). - **Signature**: 10.0 KB to 20.0 KB, JPEG/JPG format. - **Signature Dimensions**: 4.0 cm width × 2.0 cm height (exact 140 × 60 pixels). ### Key Rules to Follow: 1. Both ears and frontal face view must be clear. 2. No spectacles or sunglasses permitted, even if prescribed. 3. Candidate signature must be drawn on clean white paper with black or blue ink. 4. Capital letters are strictly prohibited for signature.
